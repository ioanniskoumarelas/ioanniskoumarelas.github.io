## 3. Cookies

This website uses cookies. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. When you first visit this website, a cookie consent banner is displayed. Only essential cookies are set before you provide consent.

**Types of cookies used:**

- **Essential cookies:** Required for the cookie consent banner to function (remembering your consent choice).
- **Analytics cookies:** Set by Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager after you provide consent (see Section 4).

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

When you post a comment, it is stored in the GitHub Discussions repository associated with this website. GitHub's privacy policy applies to this data. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

- **Right of access** (Art. 15) — request information about your stored data
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16) — request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** (Art. 17) — request deletion of your data
- **Right to restriction of processing** (Art. 18)
- **Right to data portability** (Art. 20)
- **Right to object** (Art. 21) — object to processing of your data
- **Right to withdraw consent** (Art. 7(3)) — withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ioanniskoumarelas/).
